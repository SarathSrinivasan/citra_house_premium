Our story

We wanted fruit to feel exciting again.

Home / About
The beginning

A tiny counter. A big fruit habit.

Citra House began with one simple question: why does healthy have to look sensible? We built a place where colour, flavour and care could all sit at the same table.

Today, our bar is still obsessed with the same details: ripe produce, thoughtful recipes, kind service and drinks worth remembering.

Our principles

Three things we will not compromise.

01

02

03

Curious recipes

We keep experimenting so the menu never becomes background noise.
